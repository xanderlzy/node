privacy policy

effective

this is a plain-language policy for node, a small productivity app (kanban board and notes). it is written to follow singapore’s personal data protection act 2012 (pdpa).

1. who we are

node is operated by (“we”, “us”). for any privacy question or request, including as our data protection officer contact, email .

2. what we collect

  • account details – your email address (and, if you use google sign-in, the name and profile details google shares with us). your password is handled by google firebase authentication; we never see it in readable form.
  • your content – the tasks, checklist rows, folders and notes you create.
  • sharing – if you share a note or folder, we store the email addresses you invite and the access level you give them. people with access can see the owner’s email address and the email addresses of others who have access to that item.
  • technical data – sign-in times, provider, and ip addresses that firebase records in its normal security logs.
  • on your device – node stores your board, notes, theme choice and sign-in state in your browser’s local storage and cache so it works offline. this stays on your device unless you sign in to sync.

if you use node as a guest, your content stays on your device and is not sent to us.

we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data.

3. why we use it

  • to create and secure your account;
  • to store your content and sync it across your devices;
  • to let you share notes and folders with people you invite, and to send a verification email when you sign up with an email address;
  • to respond to your questions and requests;
  • to detect abuse and comply with the law.

we use your data only for these purposes. we do not use your content to train ai models or for marketing.

4. consent

by creating an account or signing in, you consent to us collecting, using and disclosing your data as described here. you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 8). withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the sync service to you.

5. who we share it with

we use google (firebase authentication and cloud firestore) to run sign-in and store your data. google acts on our behalf under its own terms and security commitments. we do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law or to protect rights, safety or security.

6. storage outside singapore

your account data is stored in google cloud firestore, region , and google may process data in other countries. we rely on google’s contractual and technical protections so that your data receives a standard of protection comparable to the pdpa.

7. how long we keep it

we keep your account and content until you delete your account. when you do, we delete your account and your content from our live database straight away. copies may remain in google’s backups and logs for a limited period before they are overwritten. data on your own devices stays there until you sign out, delete it, or clear your browser data.

8. your rights and choices

  • access – settings → export my data downloads everything we hold in your account as a json file.
  • correction – edit your tasks and notes in the app at any time.
  • deletion / withdraw consent – settings → delete account permanently deletes your account and content. notes and folders you own are deleted for everyone you shared them with, and items others shared with you disappear from your account but stay with their owners.
  • anything else – email . we aim to reply within 30 days.

9. security

data is encrypted in transit and at rest by google, and our database rules let each signed-in user read and write only their own data. no system is perfectly secure. if a data breach affects your personal data, we will assess it promptly and notify the pdpc and affected people as the law requires.

10. children

node is not directed at children under 13, and you should not create an account if you are under 13. if you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

11. changes

we may update this policy. the effective date above shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about important changes.

12. complaints

please contact us first at so we can try to fix the problem. you may also contact the personal data protection commission (pdpc) at pdpc.gov.sg.
